Our Premise

Real estate is not just a sector.

It is a financial asset class — and ESADE students should engage with it at that level.

EREA operates at the intersection of investment, capital markets, ESG, proptech, and urban strategy. Our members engage directly with senior partners, fund managers, and developers through alumni-exclusive events before graduating.

ESADE's international profile and the depth of its Alumni Real Estate Club give EREA a reach and credibility that is difficult to replicate at any other institution in Spain.

We co-organise the annual Capital Markets Seminar with the ESADE Alumni Real Estate Club and the Urban Land Institute, and participate in The District, Spain's leading proptech and real estate investment congress.

What We Do

Events & Initiatives

All Events
Flagship Event

ESADE Real Estate Challenge

A one-week live investment case competition with senior professionals from Inmobiliaria Colonial, Anticipa (Blackstone), CBRE, and CONREN TRAMWAY. Teams build financial models, defend assumptions, and present to a jury of executives. Over 100 students have participated across editions.

FormatLive Case Competition
DurationOne Week
Scale80–100 Participants
Investment Competition

Students act as investment managers, analysing opportunities and pitching directly to industry professionals from CBRE, Anticipa (Blackstone), and Colliers.

Capital Markets Seminar

Co-organised with the ESADE Alumni Real Estate Club and Urban Land Institute. Covers capital allocation, debt markets, and ESG strategy with senior fund managers.

ESADE Housing Fair

Connecting ESADE's international student community with verified housing providers across Barcelona. Supported by Fotocasa and key local operators.

The District Visit

Annual group visit to The District, Spain's leading proptech and real estate investment congress in Barcelona. Direct access to 4,000+ industry attendees.

Alumni Network Dinners

Private dinners with ESADE Real Estate Alumni now at Blackstone, CBRE, JLL, and Inmobiliaria Colonial. Off-the-record conversations on careers and deals.

Proptech Workshop Series

Deep-dives into data analytics, digital twin technology, and ESG reporting tools reshaping institutional real estate. Co-hosted with industry partners.
