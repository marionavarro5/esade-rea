Our Premise

Real estate is not just a sector. It is a financial asset class, and ESADE students should engage with it at that level.

EREA operates at the intersection of investment, capital markets, ESG, proptech, and urban strategy. Our members engage directly with senior partners, fund managers, and developers through alumni-exclusive events before graduating.

ESADE's international profile and the depth of its Alumni Real Estate Club give EREA a reach and credibility that is difficult to replicate at any other institution in Spain.

We co-organise the annual Capital Markets Seminar with the ESADE Alumni Real Estate Club and the Urban Land Institute, and participate in The District, Spain's leading proptech and real estate investment congress.

Events and Initiatives

Flagship Event

ESADE Real Estate Challenge

A one-week live investment case competition with senior professionals from Inmobiliaria Colonial, Anticipa (Blackstone), CBRE, and CONREN TRAMWAY. Teams build financial models, defend assumptions, and present to a jury of executives. Over 100 students have participated across editions.

Format Live Case Competition
Duration One Week
Scale 80-100 Participants

Investment Competition

Students act as investment managers, analysing opportunities and pitching directly to industry professionals. Partners include Vecturie, CBRE, JLL, and Engel & Volkers.

Capital Markets Seminar

Co-organised with the ESADE Alumni Real Estate Club and Urban Land Institute, covering capital allocation, geopolitical risk, AI in real estate, and institutional investment strategy.

ESADE Housing Fair

Connecting ESADE's international student community with verified housing providers across Barcelona. Supported by Engel & Volkers, Mimeisa, and ElDomi Living, with 130+ participants.

Real Estate and the Law

A joint initiative with ESADE's Law Society covering regulatory risk in residential investment, urban planning law, and the legal structuring of institutional transactions.

Asset and Construction Visits

On-site exposure to live projects. This year: Almogavers 100 in Barcelona's 22@ district with CONREN TRAMWAY, covering ESG, office repositioning, and financing structures.

Recruitment Events

Dedicated sessions where partner firms present directly to our membership. Past participants include Macc Residential (Cerberus Capital) and CONREN TRAMWAY.
